Data & limits

Distance is great-circle, threaded around the islands and out through the fairways at Newport and Rainbow Harbor. Sail time holds a 42° beat and adds a gybing angle downwind against the wind you set — a planning figure, not a forecast. Arrival clocks use sunrise and sunset solved for each leg's own position and date.

Chart and isobaths: MapTiler. Seafloor relief: Esri Ocean, GEBCO and NOAA. Navaids: US Coast Guard Light List. Protected areas: CDFW. Traffic lanes and precautionary area: 33 CFR 167.501–503. Seabed: classified from bathymetry, so it covers every patch of water on screen. Anchorages: local knowledge over live place data, with depth, shelter and bottom measured from the same bathymetry.
